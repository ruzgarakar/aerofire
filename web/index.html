How to read this FIRMS records active fire / thermal anomaly detections. Because MODIS and VIIRS differ in spatial resolution and overpass times, detection counts must not be read directly as a number of fires. FRP (MW) measures the radiative energy output of a detected fire; it is not a measure of damage or burned area.
