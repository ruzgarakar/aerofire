How it works. (1) Fire detections from the live feed are grouped into 0.25° areas. (2) The current 10 m wind at each area comes from Open-Meteo. (3) Smoke is assumed to drift downwind within a sector of ± the chosen angle, up to wind speed × transport time (15–120 km). (4) Each settlement inside that sector gets a score from its distance to the fire and the fire power: five levels: Very high ≥ 0.70, High ≥ 0.50, Moderate ≥ 0.30, Low ≥ 0.15, otherwise Very low. If the wind is below 5 km/h only places within 10 km are flagged. Limits: this is a screening heuristic, not a smoke dispersion model; it ignores terrain, wind changes with height and over time, rain, and smoke chemistry; it does not estimate air quality or health effects. Method: rule-based; no machine learning or AI model is used. Data: settlements (villages, towns, cities) come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0, about 53,000 places); population is the value recorded there and is missing for many villages (shown as n/a), so the people total is a lower bound. Province comes from GADM 4.1 boundaries; the district is the nearest district centre within the province and is approximate.
